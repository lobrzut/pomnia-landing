what it does

Scattered conversations become one memory — and every agent gets to keep it.

Capture

Conversations from Claude Code, Cursor, Claude Desktop and Antigravity, pulled in automatically. Exports from Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok — and your PDFs, DOCX, EPUBs — imported on demand. Conversation and document blobs sit in one vault folder under AES-256-GCM — portable as a folder; knowledge sidecars stay local plaintext (see privacy).

Editor profiles and assistant configs are snapshotted into the same vault — preserved, not scattered across machines. Pushing them back into a fresh install is on the roadmap, not in the box.

Distill

A local model turns raw chat into knowledge: decisions, fixes, dead ends worth remembering. Runs on your GPU via Ollama — no mandatory cloud, no telemetry, no third pair of eyes.

Recall

Any MCP-speaking agent can open the session already knowing you — your stack, your decisions, that bug you fixed in March. One question: search_library. One answer, from everything you've ever discussed.

A note costs about a thousand tokens to write, a few hundred to read back. Set that against what you spend every morning re-explaining your stack and re-deriving what you already fixed. Plain markdown on your disk — do the arithmetic yourself.

Claude Code Cursor Antigravity → pomnia → Claude Desktop VS Code …

Chats are read from the assistants that keep them in a readable store; the distilled memory flows back out to any MCP-speaking agent — over MCP, the protocol they all already speak. After one Connect snippet in your client, recall is just search_library — no per-vendor memory plugins.

why local

Vendor memories are silos. Yours shouldn't be.

ChatGPT's memory won't follow you to Cursor. Claude's won't follow you anywhere else either. Each vendor remembers a version of you — on their servers, in their product, and you can't take it with you.

Pomnia inverts that: the memory lives with you on your machine — chat/doc blobs in an AES vault; distilled notes and the search index as local files you control — and the agents come to it. Switch tools any day; your context stays — growing a little sharper with every conversation you feed it.

One memory. Every agent.
On your disk.
