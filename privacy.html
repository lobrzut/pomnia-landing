Privacy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This site collects nothing. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixel, no fonts loaded from someone else's server, no form to fill in. There is no account to create and no newsletter to join. You are reading a static page.

Don't take my word for it

A privacy claim you cannot verify is just marketing. This one takes about ten seconds:

Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, and reload this page. Every request should be to pomnia.ai and nothing else. Then view the page source and search for http — the only outbound links point to GitHub, and they are links, not loads.

If you ever find that this stops being true, tell me and I will either fix it or change this page. Both of those are better than the sentence quietly becoming false.

What about server logs?

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web server, their infrastructure processes the technical data needed to deliver a page — your IP address, the browser you use, and what you requested. I do not add analytics on top of that, and I do not build profiles from it. This is a hosting necessity, not a data collection choice.

The application itself

Pomnia stores your vault on your own machine, or on a server you run. Chat and document blobs are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a key derived from a passphrase that is never stored anywhere — lose it and those blobs are unrecoverable, including by me. Sidecar files that travel with the vault folder (skills/, USER.md, sessions/, distilled notes) and the local Brain search index (library.db under %AppData%\pomnia\brain-core-data\ on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/pomnia/brain-core-data/ on macOS, ~/.config/pomnia/brain-core-data/ on Linux) are plaintext on disk by design — protect those folders like any other local notes.

„How is this encrypted if the index is outside the vault?”

Honest answer: the passphrase encrypts the archive (raw chat snapshots and document blobs). It does not encrypt the knowledge agents search. Distilled notes are readable markdown beside the vault header; library.db stores full chunk text plus embeddings so search stays fast without unlocking every blob. That is a deliberate trade-off, not a hidden full mirror of sealed chats — but disk access to those paths can read recalled memory without the passphrase.

PL: Hasło chroni archiwum (snapshoty + bloby dokumentów). Nie chroni pamięci wyszukiwanej przez agentów — notatki distill i library.db są plaintext. Dostęp do dysku = treść wiedzy bez hasła.

What lock does and does not do: locking clears the passphrase-derived key from memory; encrypted blobs stay sealed. It does not wipe library.db or the plaintext sidecars. Uninstall may leave AppData behind — delete that folder if you want the search index gone.

Distillation runs against your own Ollama instance. No vault content is ever transmitted to me, because there is no server of mine for it to be transmitted to. If you point Pomnia at a cloud model instead of a local one, the content you send goes to that provider under their terms — that is your choice, and it is off by default.

If you email me

Mail sent to hello@pomnia.ai arrives in a mailbox hosted by cyber_Folks S.A. in Poland. I keep correspondence for as long as it is useful to answer you, and you can ask me to delete it. That is the only personal data connected with this project, and it exists only because you chose to write to me.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask what data is held about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. Since the only thing that can exist is an email you sent me, that request is easy to satisfy. Write to hello@pomnia.ai.

Pomnia is built by an independent developer based in Poland, who is the controller of anything you send to that address. If you think I have handled something badly, you can complain to the Polish supervisory authority, UODO.

Changes

If this ever stops being accurate, the date above changes and the text changes with it. I will not quietly edit a claim out of existence.
